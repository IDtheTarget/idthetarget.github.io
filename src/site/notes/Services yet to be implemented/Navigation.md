I use Google Maps **all** the time to not just navigate, but to find businesses and their information (are they open, etc.)  I hear good things about [Organic Maps](https://organicmaps.app/), which relies on [Open Street Maps](https://www.openstreetmap.org/), but a quick couple of queries shows several missing addresses to which I travel often.

I tried [OsmAnd](https://osmand.net), but while it had a little more info that Organic Maps, it still didn't have what I'd need to rely upon it.

[HERE WeGo](https://wego.here.com/) had those addresses, and even some business info.

I haven't yet looked at [Magic Earth](https://www.magicearth.com/)

Ultimately it's going to come down to privacy vs. usability. I'm not yet sure where I'm going to come down on this issue.
